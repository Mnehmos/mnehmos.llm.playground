# Evidence and limitations for owner and reviewer

**Does the program break?** In frozen arms, no. Checkpoint replay scores the parameter program separately from the full network. A programmed margin of one can be overwhelmed by a learned competing margin. Attenuation exactly accounts for non-tied outcomes at the current checkpoint. This is algebraic verification. The separate prospective slope forecast did not improve the measured confusion counts over step-50 authority.

**Is cross-entropy necessary?** Ordinary conversion confidence gradients were sufficient in the Experiment 29 replay. In Experiment 32 the routed arm permanently zeroed that direct gradient and retained only 8.7% final authority. An outside-domain task learned through those same rows. Shared learning is another route to displacement. Controlled Experiment 31 replay showed retained optimizer state can also move a suppressed readout without fresh gradients, but resetting state did not prevent recurrence. We do not infer exact historical credit from token gradients.

**Does soft protection solve preservation?** The matched intervention improves authority by 87.2 points, paired 95% CI 84.4 to 89.7 points and Holm p=.00195. Mean authority is 98.6% and the same readout learns the flagged task at 92.6%. Eight of twelve meet all criteria on both ordinary and interventional outputs and the flagged task. The supervised objective remains active through the 300-step window. Removing it, broader plasticity, and long-term preservation are untested. Scale and satisfied margin each pass three seeds. Hard-bounded and isolated cases have a domain-specific margin guarantee, not a learned proof of concept use elsewhere.

**How independent are the data?** The Experiment 30 final panel used nine written base questions. The new conversion endpoint has 52 written bases (13 groups of four aliases), 1,365 eligible draws and 1,119 unique pairs. Aliases stay in the same split. The automaton terminal panel has 384 words, 246 eligible bases, 2,632 eligible draws and 2,604 unique pairs. Same-machine process separation protects held assignment before the terminal gate. It is not third-party custody. Seeds, not pairs, are the units of inference. Reported uncertainty is conditional on these task suites.

**Does the second task replicate the strongest failure?** It replicates the dissociation with lesser severity. Frozen-only automaton authority was 94.6%, conditional authority 94.5%, exact isolated authority, and unpatched hits 2.9%. Donor truth, unchanged truth and the reversed-suffix alternative are separated from the intended class. The trainable arm remains exact on full authority and nearly exact in isolation. Binary parity retains its original limitations and is not overwritten.

**Other limitations.** Reserved capacity is required for the trained-host demonstration. Experiment 31 durability reused Experiment 30's released questions and is exploratory. Suppression and attenuation are large within edited blocks. The flagged task is simple and renewed loss after removing soft protection is not tested. Weak force performance does not establish compositional reuse. The results concern finite additive hybrids and do not generalize to large language models, arbitrary installation, or natural concept lifecycles.
