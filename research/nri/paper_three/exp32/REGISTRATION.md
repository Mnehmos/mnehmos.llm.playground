## Designs and estimands

The objective experiment uses 12 matched seeds 832301–832312, six arms, and 300 AdamW updates. The original recipe is ordinary CE. Satisfied replaces only that natural conversion loss with max(0,0.5 minus the full winning margin). Scale multiplies the installed decoder logits by five while leaving its prediction and state unchanged. Routed detaches the residual from the natural conversion loss only; it remains active and trainable through the flagged extra task. Soft adds four times a hinge with target margin 0.5 on swapped canonical states. Matched ordinary adds the same weighted hinge on ordinary states, with the same forward passes, extra sample count and shared draw stream. All arms train the flagged task using CE on a different flag token with the written value as target, and auxiliary source-group routing using its own output rows. Identical residual parameterization and dimensions are used in all arms. Soft does not clip weights or logits and does not promise preservation on other objectives or domains.

The automaton experiment uses 12 other matched seeds 832401–832412, five arms, and 300 updates. Its exact programmed state uses 5-dimensional one-hot vectors and four learned-shape 5x5 permutation or partial-reset tensors, written without gradient fitting. The transitions in symbol order are [1,0,3,2,4], [2,1,4,0,3], [0,0,1,3,2], [4,2,0,1,3] for states 0–4. Two transitions fail to commute and the third is non-injective. Every start state and every six-token word was exhaustively validated against an integer-table oracle. The prefix state after token three is replaced with the donor prefix state; the original suffix and raw path stay fixed. The frozen/trainable unrestricted comparison and bounded/disabled reference arms reproduce the earlier architecture. Soft is a prospectively declared transfer of the independently qualified conversion objective. The soft-versus-frozen contrast tests the transferred package, without a separate matched extra-supervision control in this task.

Both tasks use a two-layer additive competing transformer with model dimension 16, two heads and MLP dimension 32; the readout starts at zero. AdamW uses LR .002 and PyTorch defaults. Draw 40 natural, 40 extra and 40 donor IDs per update within each seed stream, and use the same base draws for flagged and routing tasks. Checkpoints are 0,100,200,300 with authored reviews before continuation; record the early margin panels at 1,5,10,20,50. Scopes are intentionally limited to 300 updates. Additional post-repair durability is covered by Experiment 31. No treatment is changed after this lock.

## Data and evaluation

Conversion has 468 written prompts (13 residues, three source and destination scales, four style IDs). Hold out semantic triples as groups so style aliases cannot straddle splits. The reserved union is source 1 with value at least 6 or output unit 2, or source 2 with output unit 0. Shuffle the held triples and split into thirds, by group. Automaton partitions are a shuffle of the 4,096 distinct sequences with 384 each for visible, hidden development and final, and the remaining 2,944 for training. A separate local process holds the private salt and held split allocation in memory. Release its final and hidden-dev scores together after audit gates. This is same-machine process separation, without external administration. No reserved prompts enter training draws. The known legal transition tables may encode the mathematical rules of those prompts.

Donors come only from the training pool, with 128 initial draws per base for conversion and 64 for automaton. Count only samples with distinct intended, unchanged truth, donor truth and wrong alternative. The conversion alternative treats the donor written number as a base-unit number in the base source unit. The automaton alternative reverses the suffix transition order.
