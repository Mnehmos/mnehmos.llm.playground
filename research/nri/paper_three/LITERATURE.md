# Paper Three · Related work and claim boundary

**Weight-space programming.** Li et al., *WeightCode* (2026), install computations into trained language, navigation and image networks through executable edits that read and write weights without gradient-based fitting. The paper tests selective gate ablations, cross-network reruns and preservation through a second executable weight program. Its retention experiment concerns successive installations. Its fine-tuning comparisons evaluate alternate ways of reaching the target behavior. Neither directly supplies the proposed longitudinal contrast between the isolated correctness of an installed program and its intervention authority while that same installed model continues to train. Experiment 29 installed into a fresh architecture with explicit reserved routes. A pretrained-host follow-up is needed before treating it as evidence about post-training installation. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.25476v1)

**Residual bypass and intervenability.** Marcou et al. (2026) explicitly identify an unrestricted residual as a route that can compromise concept interventions. Their solution models learned residuals jointly with concepts and propagates interventions through statistical conditioning. Tapli et al. identify leakage and bottleneck bypass as issues in concept-bottleneck evaluation. Laguna et al.'s *Beyond Concept Bottleneck Models* studies how to make pretrained classifiers intervenable. These results rule out treating bypass or weakened interventions as novel. Our proposed contrast directly tests a known installed mathematical operation, freeze-only versus route protection, during further optimization. [Res-SCBM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23421) · [Rethinking CBMs](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Tapli_Rethinking_Concept_Bottleneck_Models_From_Pitfalls_to_Solutions_CVPR_2026_paper.html) · [Beyond CBMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13544)

**Causal abstraction and supervision.** Geiger et al. (2021) use interchange interventions to test agreement with an abstract causal model. Geiger et al. (2022) train with that intervention objective. Laguna et al. also fine-tune for intervenability. The supervision used here is an application of IIT to a known program interface. The potential result is preservation under competing learnable paths, tested against matched extra ordinary supervision. A low raw residual magnitude is diagnostic evidence. The primary result requires correct interventional outputs. [Causal Abstractions](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/4f5c422f4d49a5a807eda27434231040-Abstract.html) · [IIT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/geiger22a.html)

**Compiled mechanisms, competing circuits, and retention.** Tracr compiles specified algorithms into transformer parameters for mechanistic ground truth. The sparse-parity study of Merrill et al. tests changing dominance between learned subnetworks during grokking. Geirhos et al. describe shortcut learning in terms of benchmark success and failed transfer. Cloud et al. show gradient masking can localize learned computation. Kirkpatrick et al. test retention through differential constraints on weight movement. Meng et al. use causal tracing and targeted edits for factual recall. Together they motivate controlling both optimization and information flow, separating retention of function from retention of its route to the output, and measuring consequences of targeted repairs. None of these cited results establishes that the conjunction in Paper Three is new across the full literature. [Tracr](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05062) · [Two Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11873) · [Shortcut learning](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-020-00257-z) · [Gradient routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04332) · [EWC](https://doi.org/10.1073/PNAS.1611835114) · [ROME](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05262)

Keep the priority claim provisional. The targeted search found the above close precedents and no exact match for the installed-program, post-training-integrity and intervention-authority factorial comparison. That search result is insufficient for a first-ever claim. Use “causal displacement” as an operational term for this measured dissociation and selective rescue.
