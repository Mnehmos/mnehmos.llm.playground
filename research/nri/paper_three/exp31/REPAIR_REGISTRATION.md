# Experiment 31.1 · Durable causal repair
Use a fixed, exploratory three-seed subset, the first three numeric Experiment 30 seeds (830701, 830703, 830709). Use displaced A, B, D and F endpoints and authorize copies only after matching their terminal checkpoint SHA-256. This study deliberately reuses the released Experiment 30 evaluation and is exploratory. Repair/tuning consumes training pairs only. Run 96 continuation copies for 600 updates, saving full audits every 100 including zero and observing 1/5/10/20/50. Primary summaries immediate, 100/300/600; publish all intermediate margins, isolated and ordinary outcomes, routing and force. Report means, seed-level ranges, and matched changes rather than high-powered inference.

Within each matched source use untouched, zero the competing conversion readout, retain one eighth of it, norm-budgeted intervention fit, unbudgeted causal-supervision repair, and the same causal repair followed by soft residual protection. Also cross untouched and zeroing with resetting AdamW state. Reset retains the original training RNG and uses the same learning rate and optimizer configuration. All copies follow their original A/B/D/F objectives during continuation, except the protected copy adds the mean squared centered conversion residual penalty with coefficient 1. It has no hard clamp or architectural guarantee. Zeroing is a reversible one-time edit. It does not disable further learning in that block.

Causal-supervision repair fits only the conversion readout for 50 updates using fresh Adam at .003 on 96 fixed-stream training interchanges per step plus all ordinary training conversions, with frozen upstream features. Norm-budgeted repair is identical except it projects onto a radius of 10% of the original conversion readout norm after each step. Repair phase optimizer is discarded; continuation retains original AdamW moments by default. Report absolute, global relative, and block-relative changes measured at step zero. Immediate recovery requires at least 95% authority and a 15-point gain over untouched; durable requires at least 95% at 100, 300, and 600. An early drop can coexist with recovery at the endpoint. Compare zero-retained with zero-reset to test influence of the optimizer state as a whole, not individual AdamW mechanisms. Seed IDs retain pairing.
