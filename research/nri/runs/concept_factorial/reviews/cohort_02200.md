**Conceptual correction to my own instrument interpretation.** A "number" reading under the patch (donor value
converted along the base route) is *also* what a reusable rule over raw numbers would produce: it indicates which
variable the code carries, not lookup versus rule. The registered "utilised" onset measures specifically the
canonical variable, which is what C was supervised toward. Lookup vs rule is measured behaviourally (held-out) and,
causally, by the novel-combination patch. Seed 2008 is a counterexample to "canonical reading is necessary for
composition"; whether it is sufficient-but-not-necessary will be assessed across all composing networks at the end.
