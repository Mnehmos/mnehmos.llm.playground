# G05 Session 14: Native neural causal topology

## Scope
Actual frozen 69,831-parameter transformers from G03, exact historical parents as archived in S12. No weight changes to any backbone. The observer receives architecture-defined intervention ports, not semantic role assignments. Native donor-output transfer is the target; donor output can itself be mathematically wrong. An absent singleton *target witness* is not evidence of zero singleton effect. This is a retrospective study on historical familiar domains; observer transfer is across excluded backbone identities, not blind native mathematical generalization.

## Five-port atlas
1920 matched source/donor prompt pairs (384 per backbone, changing one legal field); independently patch four attention head contributions and one skip+bias component at each of the two attention layers. Layer1 patch spans all token positions, then layer2 is recomputed; layer2 patch acts at final token. Both decomposed endpoint argmaxes match the actual native outputs on every pair; maximum observed logit reconstruction difference 8.49e-5 (float32). All 32 masks measured per pair/layer (122880 mask-pair evaluations). 1575 pairs have different native predictions, giving 3150 eligible layer-pair comparisons. 1573 lack a singleton target witness; minimum cardinalities 1:1577, 2:994, 3:421, 4:127, 5:31. 618 have multiple inclusion-minimal sets; 595 exhibit a successful subset with an unsuccessful superset. Both source and donor are mathematically correct in 1422 comparisons, of which 759 lack a singleton target witness. These are not independent networks or estimates of population prevalence.

Two shared 961-parameter scorers trained on 39300 candidate masks from 34001 exact/quotient only, 600 updates each. Input includes native activation delta statistics and first-order target margin estimates from 13 logit gradients; no mixed-mask forward results or semantic classes used for ranking. Three other frozen backbones reserved for policy transfer. Each policy examines at most eight proper nonempty subset proposals then checks every proper subset of the first successful proposal; only additional subset evaluations counted, endpoints already known. All results are replayed counterfactual policies over a fully measured atlas in this stage, not online timing. Unresolved penalty 32. On 1840 eligible held-out layer-pair cases, random 1649 witnesses, mean calls 9.4364, mean penalized 12.7582; linear-cost 1203, 4.4630, 15.5413; cost-aware neural A 1791, 4.1533, 5.0054; B 1784, 4.0467, 5.0207. Full certification 1840/1840, 30 additional calls, globally minimal. Neural A/B achieve globally minimum cardinality 1773/1768 of 1840. Probability-only neural policies find 1814 each but incur more certification calls. Inference requires derivative information and known native endpoints; query counts are not total compute.

## Nine-port cross-layer transfer
Fresh 480 pairs, first96 of separate registered streams. Joint ports: layer1 four heads plus skip, layer2 four heads; layer2 unpatched heads recomputed after layer1 intervention. Full and layer1-full transfer verified. All 512 masks measured (245760 mask-pair evaluations). 387 eligible pairs, 133 lack singleton witness; 13 have smaller cross-layer witness than either restricted layer-only set (with the same nine-port definition, no independent layer2 skip). Frozen observer transfer, no retraining. On 226 held-out-lineage cases random finds all 226 at 50.1681 queries; cost-aware A 221 at 6.1106, B 222 at 5.4027. Registered penalty512 yields 17.4381 and 14.4646 vs random 50.1681. Global minima achieved in 209 and 211 cases. Probability-only neural selectors find all 226 but certify large sets at 229-239 calls. Exact fallback needed for complete reliability.

Real online run on 57 eligible held-out cases from fixed first24 source pairs per model: all three methods produce valid inclusion-minimal witnesses. Exact exhaustive: 510 additional queries, mean 9.058 ms, 57 global minima. Ascending-cardinality batches: 23.3158 queries, 0.903 ms, 57 global minima. Frozen neural A with exact fallback: 8.1053 queries, 6.857 ms, 54 global minima. Mean neural feature time 5.656 ms (13 gradients at each of two layers, plus features). Counts exclude common base/donor extraction; timing includes actual forward and scoring operations, single-thread CPU, fixed method order, one trial per case. Not evidence of neural CPU superiority; batched cardinality is much faster. The live policy uses no atlas information for selection; offline atlas checks the result afterward.

## Neuron-level causal topology
Native 256 post-ReLU MLP activations are separately patchable, with residual fixed. Exact algebraic logit difference decomposes as sum of neuron contributions (computed in float32). Sorting individual gains to the donor-vs-base margin produces a minimum-cardinality subset for *strict pairwise* margin crossing; equality/roundoff boundary not treated as crossing. 1574/1575 eligible pairs cross; mean minimum 2.184, max9. Only 576/1575 also attain full multiclass donor argmax at that size (random size-matched subset: 1/1574 reachable). Full neuron-only patch attains donor output 955/1575. The residual bypass and other competing logits matter. Do not conflate pairwise margin certification with multiclass sufficiency or a globally smallest multiclass coalition.

## Audit and reproduction
`topology.py` followed by `joint.py`, then `audit.py`, in a clean S14 directory adjacent to exact extracted `nri_G05_session12` (core archive). S12 supplies `sources/nri_G05_session09` and PARENT_MANIFEST.sha256. S12 SHA-256 checked on 36 parent-manifest entries. Use Python with PyTorch, numpy and pandas, CPU, one thread. Code preserves primary/replay weights, optimizer and RNG every100 steps; 14 bitwise replay comparisons, 14 primary states, 14 replay states. Audit checks all 16560 five-port and 3096 nine-port search transactions and 16951 accepted certificates, plus 171 online transactions recorded. Exact checkpoint states are never reconstructed. The archive includes all mask maps, raw pair data, search traces and checkpoints. Scripts use fixed seeds and fail rather than silently reusing nonempty training dirs. Timing results are inherently non-bitwise. Training and qualification data were computed in the same process, with only named training-lineage masks used for optimization and train-only feature normalization. There is no external sealed examiner. This was a bounded CPU pilot. The reference output is the native donor prediction, not necessarily correct mathematics. Head and neuron intervention locations are architectural choices; actual grouping/search is learned only at mask-ranking stage. Further work should learn when a causal observation warrants a targeted update and optimize end-to-end compute against strong batched exact controls, including multiclass neuron localization.
